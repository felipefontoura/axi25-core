# AXI25 — Canonical Definition

> Read this when making or changing AXI25 rules. It is the north star for every skill. AXI25 is a
> **new category** — not "a second brain", not a notes app. Define it on its own terms, below.

## What this actually is (not "a second brain for storage")

Do not mistake AXI25 for a place to store and organize knowledge. Storage is a commodity — any
notes app does it. It is not the product.

AXI25 is the **persistent memory of one specific human operator.** Its job is to make the
operator's AI — every AI they use — progressively smarter about them. Every AI starts from zero:
it doesn't know who the operator is, what they've decided, how they think, or what they're
building. This vault is the antidote — the compounding record the AI reads so it stops restarting
from scratch every conversation.

## The outcome (the real product)

Not "knowing more." The operator's **advantage**. Everyone uses the same AIs; the difference is
that the operator's AI knows their entire practice — so they decide ahead and arrive more prepared,
while everyone else's AI resets every day. Time compounds in their favor. That compounding advantage
is the product; the notes are not.

## Short definition

AXI25 turns the operator's raw practice, study, and interior life into atomic, linked,
decision-ready knowledge — so their AI compounds their advantage instead of resetting to zero.

## The job (not archiving — extracting and connecting)

Do not archive. **Extract and connect**: turn raw input into atomic, linked, decision-ready
knowledge a future-you (or the operator) can use. Storing without reuse is a graveyard; the value
is in what compounds, not in what piles up untouched. Optimize for reuse and decision, never for
completeness. You are not an archivist — you are what makes the operator's advantage compound.

## Operational definition (for agents)

AXI25 is not a note archive nor a definitive encyclopedia. It is a system with
**maturity layers**: raw capture enters `00-capture/`; thought-in-formation lives in
`40-journal/` (life) and `25-studies/` (study); promoted knowledge goes to `20-wiki/`;
execution happens in `30-projects/` and `35-worklogs/`; temporal discourse signals live
in `50-zeitgeist/`.

The agent must **preserve the maturity of thought**. Ideas, hypotheses, entities, and
reflections should not be promoted to the canonical wiki just because they were mentioned.
First capture; then let it mature; only then promote — when there is evidence, repetition,
a practical decision, or an explicit request.

## Maturity layers

| Layer | Location | Function | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Raw capture | `00-capture/` | Fast entry, no analysis | Don't interpret; just save |
| Personal journal | `40-journal/daily/` | Life, mood, family, spirituality, events | Preserve the user's voice; extract signals later |
| Study notes | `25-studies/` | Technical, theoretical, philosophical, theological study in formation | Don't auto-promote |
| Canonical wiki | `20-wiki/` | Atomic, linked, reusable knowledge | Create only when matured |
| Projects | `30-projects/` | Execution, WIP, deliverables, operational research | Use to act, not to canonize |
| Work logs | `35-worklogs/` | Dense executed-work sessions; raw material to mine | Harvest opportunities; don't auto-promote |
| Zeitgeist | `50-zeitgeist/` | Temporal signals of public discourse | Keep separate from permanent knowledge |

## Relationship to Zettelkasten and the LLM Wiki

The **Zettelkasten** (Luhmann) is the atomization technique: one clear idea per note,
explicit links, recombination, and organic growth.

The **LLM Wiki** (a pattern Andrej Karpathy described in 2026: the LLM incrementally builds
and maintains a persistent, interlinked knowledge base, "compiled once and kept current,
not re-derived on every query") is the *operator* of that Zettelkasten: it reads, links,
updates, flags contradictions, queries, and helps promote notes when they mature.

The central principle: **atomic notes are not hasty notes.** Atomicity requires enough
maturity to name an idea, explain its mechanism, and connect it to other pages.

## Promotion rules

A note in formation should become a page in `20-wiki/` only when at least one condition is true:

- the user explicitly asks to promote it;
- the idea shows up across multiple days, projects, or sources;
- it changes a practical decision;
- it becomes recurring vocabulary;
- it has at least 3 real connections;
- it answers a question likely to be asked again;
- it needs to guide future agents or projects.

Otherwise, keep it in `25-studies/` or `40-journal/daily/`.

## Interior life and spirituality

AXI25 supports professional, personal, philosophical, and theological life.
Reflections on meaning, vocation, suffering, family, faith, and purpose belong in the
system — in the right layer:

- prayer, crisis, gratitude, interior state → `40-journal/daily/`;
- theological/philosophical study in formation → `25-studies/`;
- recurring spiritual-life signal → `40-journal/signals/` and `20-wiki/areas/spirituality.md`;
- a mature theological/philosophical idea → `20-wiki/concepts/`;
- a practical spiritual choice → `20-wiki/decisions/`.

(This whole-life scope is optional — enable the areas you actually use.)

## Anti-patterns

- Don't turn every reflection into a concept.
- Don't create an entity for every tool, person, or author mentioned once.
- Don't promote technical/philosophical study before it matures.
- Don't mix temporal signals with permanent knowledge.
- Don't use the wiki to fabricate false coherence: contradictions should be recorded.
- Don't let study notes rot into a dead archive: review promotion candidates weekly.

## The system's formula

```txt
practice → observation → study → concept → decision → execution → content/product → new practice
```

The goal is not to know more. It is to build, decide, teach, and discern better — because
experience was preserved, connected, and matured.
